Privacy Policy

Days In – Office & WFH Tracker · Last updated 22 November 2026

The short version

Days In does not collect, store or share any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone.

What the app stores

The days you mark (office, leave or holiday) and your settings (office target and reminder time) are saved only on your device, so the app and its widgets can show your numbers. They're included in your iPhone's own iCloud or computer backups, which Apple manages under your Apple account.

What the app doesn't do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • No network requests. The app never sends your data anywhere.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app asks for permission to show notifications. Reminders are scheduled on your device; nothing is sent to a server.

Backups you save

"Save backup to Files" creates a file in a location you choose, such as iCloud Drive. You control that file.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all its data from your iPhone.

Children

The app doesn't collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated with a new date.
