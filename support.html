Days In

WFH tracker for iPhone · Plan your hybrid month

Getting started

Tap a weekday to mark it as a day in. Press and hold a day to mark it as WFH, leave or a public holiday. Swipe the calendar to change month.

How the % works

Only weekdays count. Leave and holidays are left out. An unmarked past weekday counts as WFH; today counts as WFH from 6 AM unless you mark it. Days in you mark in the future are "planned" and count towards With plan.

Moving to a new iPhone

Your days come with you in your iPhone backup. You can also go to Settings → Save backup to Files, then Restore from Files on the new phone.
